# 1 High Signal To Noise Ratio
* We should strive for simplicity.   

 ## Positive bar (what “good” looks like)

- New information or a **non-obvious** angle per paragraph (or tight justification for density).
- **Concrete** nouns: systems, interfaces, metrics, owners, timelines—not only abstractions.
- Tradeoffs tied to **this** problem, not generic “pros/cons of microservices.”
- Compression: could you remove a paragraph without losing a decision? If not, good.

## Negative patterns (failures of substance)

- **Padding** — repeats the prompt, repeats headings, circular restatements.
- **Universal advice** — could apply to any company or any system without edit.
- **Section-shaped emptiness** — headers with boilerplate that adds no new claim.
- **Obvious listicles** — truisms masquerading as depth.
- **Hollow confidence** — strong recommendations with no criteria or verification path.

## Surface tells (secondary—use as evidence of low density)

Buzzword stacks, fake symmetry (“on one hand… on the other” with no decision), template voice, vague names (`handler`, `data`, `utils` without role), authority tone without criteria. Cite these **after** naming the **substance** failure they support.

## Output discipline

- Lead findings with **what is missing or weak in value**, not with smell words alone.
